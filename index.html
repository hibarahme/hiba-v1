Try adding or removing items below. The basket updates across all screens.

Screen layout

Each screen follows the same order: tracker, add-ons, delivery progress, then the main button. Item names, prices and the $10 minimum stay consistent.

How items are chosen

  • Returning Fresh customers: Buy again, from their past Fresh orders
  • Then rows by category, from the brief’s top-up basket: chips & snacks, drinks, ice cream & chocolate
  • First-time customers see the category rows without Buy again
  • Brands paying per click (with a minimum monthly spend) rank first in their row, with a small “Sponsored” label
  • Only items in stock at the Fresh dark store that serves the address are shown; a row with too few is hidden
  • Nothing is matched to the food order in V1
  • Copy states the goal (“Add $X, get $0 delivery”), and checkout shows free delivery at $0.00

Before launch

  • Fresh order history and stock
  • Fee rules: $10 minimum (break-even $8 at 25% margin, assumed $2 delivery cost)
  • Experiment tools and click logging for CPC billing

Prototype only, not the live Toters app. Item names, prices and delivery fees are examples. The $10 minimum uses Toters’ $8–10 top-up range, roughly 25% margin and an estimated $2 delivery cost.
